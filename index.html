ASSET GUIDE

How to Add Real Firework Videos & Audios

Clean Asset Architecture: EcoDiwali Next comes equipped with 10 procedural audio recordings and synchronized visual simulations. To play actual live real-world prerecorded videos, simply drop your MP4 files into the assets/videos/ folder!

1. Video Folder & Naming Structure

Place your MP4 files in assets/videos/ using these exact filenames:

assets/videos/ ├── rocket.mp4 ├── anar.mp4 ├── chakri.mp4 ├── flower-pot.mp4 ├── phuljhadi.mp4 ├── 100-wala-garland.mp4 (Dedicated continuous longer video) ├── sky-shot.mp4 ├── ground-spinner.mp4 ├── crackling.mp4 └── grand-finale.mp4

2. Recommended Video Specs for Android & Chrome

  • Format: MP4 (H.264 Baseline or Main profile, AAC audio).
  • Resolution: 1080×1920 (Portrait) or 1920×1080 (Landscape).
  • Framerate: 30 fps or 60 fps.
  • Preload: Metadata only (configured automatically to save mobile data).

3. Recommended Free Legal Footage Sources

Download free, royalty-free CC0 Diwali and firework videos from:

  • Pexels Fireworks Videos (Free for commercial/personal use)
  • Pixabay Diwali Footage (Free license)
  • Wikimedia Commons Fireworks Footage
Pro Tip: EcoDiwali Next automatically detects when genuine video footage is present and seamlessly plays it! If an asset is missing or still being replaced, it gracefully plays the synchronized fallback with zero broken UI.
